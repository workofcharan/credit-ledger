What this system does

Two questions, kept separate on purpose

"Can they repay?" and "Is this really them?" are different questions with different consequences when you get them wrong. Answering them with one blended score is how genuine applicants end up rejected alongside actual fraud.

📊

Explainable credit score

A transparent, rule-based score across six cash-flow and stability factors. Every point is traceable — no black box.

🛡️

Independent fraud score

Identity verification, device reputation, location consistency, and repeat-application detection — scored separately from creditworthiness.

🙅

No automated rejections

The system can only auto-approve clean cases. Everything else — including high fraud risk — goes to a human officer, never a silent decline.

🧮

Financial structuring

An editable EMI schedule, moratorium option, and margin-based break-even view, so an approval comes with a repayment plan the business can sustain.

Process

How an application moves through the system

1

Officer enters the application

Business, cash-flow, and identity/fraud signals — captured in one guided form.

2

Two independent scores are generated

A credit score and a fraud score, each with a full factor-by-factor explanation.

3

Clean cases auto-approve; everything else is referred

Only applications clean on both scores are approved automatically. Every other case — thin file, noisy signal, or genuine fraud concern — goes to the review queue with a recommendation, not a verdict.
