Help & FAQ

How do I get access?

Officers log in with an officer code issued by their branch or district office. If you're new, use Register here from the login page to create an account. For this demo, a shared demo account is available: Officer Code DEMO001, Password Demo@123.

Why was an application referred instead of approved or declined?

The system never issues an automatic decline. Anything that isn't a clean auto-approval — a borderline credit score, an unverifiable reference, a flagged device — is sent to the Review Queue with a full explanation and a recommendation. An officer makes the final call.

What's the difference between the credit score and the fraud score?

The credit score answers "can they repay?" — it's about cash flow and business stability. The fraud score answers "is this application genuine?" — identity match, device, location, and repeat-application signals. They're intentionally kept separate; see the About page for the full methodology.

Can I change a decision after it's been made?

Auto-approved and officer-finalised cases are kept as a permanent audit record on the History page. If you need to correct one, submit a fresh assessment and note the correction in the decision note field.

How do I compare two applicants?

From the History page, tick the checkbox next to up to three applicants, then open the Compare page.

How do I get a PDF of a report?

Open any application's report page and use the Download PDF Report button in the top right — it generates a real, multi-page PDF you can save or print, not just a browser print dialog.
